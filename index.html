Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about booking, services, and payment.

How do I book an appointment? —
Choose a date, select a doctor and service, and enter your details.
You'll get an SMS reminder before your visit.
Book this now
What services do you offer? +
We offer general dentistry, aesthetic dentistry, dentures, crowns & bridges, wisdom tooth removal, and orthodontic braces.
How much do treatments cost? +
Costs depend on the specific treatment required. We provide clear, transparent pricing during consultation prior to starting any procedure.
Can I pay for braces in installments? +
Yes, flexible monthly payment options are available for orthodontic braces treatment.
What if I need to reschedule or miss my appointment? +
You can easily reschedule or cancel by contacting us directly through phone or Messenger at least 24 hours in advance.
What should I bring to my first visit? +
Please bring a valid ID and any previous dental records or X-rays if you have them.

About Us

Our Story

Since 2010, MB Pagaduan Dental Care and Orthodontics has been dedicated to providing reliable and affordable dental care for every family. We take pride in being a trusted oral health partner for patients of all ages, maintaining our strong commitment to quality, accessibility, and dependable service throughout the years.

From routine dental cleanings and custom dentures to specialized orthodontic braces, we offer a comprehensive range of treatments to meet your oral health needs. Our priority is to create a welcoming and stress-free environment, ensuring that every visit to our clinic is as comfortable, pleasant, and seamless as possible.
